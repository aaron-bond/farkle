How to Play

  • Roll all 6 dice to start your turn.
  • You must set aside at least one scoring die from every roll before you're allowed to roll again.
  • Roll Again to re-roll what's left and risk it, or Pass to bank your turn score and end your turn.
  • Hot dice: use up all 6 dice and you get to roll all 6 fresh again, keeping your turn score.
  • Farkle: roll with no scoring dice at all and you lose everything banked so far this turn - not just the bad roll, the whole turn.
  • First to bank at or past the target wins immediately - there's no final round for the other player.
  • Target score depends on difficulty: Easy 1500 / Medium 3000 / Hard 5000. Higher difficulty also makes the AI opponent play more aggressively.
